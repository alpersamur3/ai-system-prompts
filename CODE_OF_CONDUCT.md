# Code of Conduct

## 🤝 Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone contributing to or using this repository, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## 📋 Our Standards

### Positive Behavior
Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Respectful Communication**: Using welcoming and inclusive language
- **Constructive Feedback**: Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- **Collaboration**: Focusing on what is best for the community and research
- **Empathy**: Showing empathy towards other community members
- **Accountability**: Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- **Learning**: Being open to learning from mistakes

### Unacceptable Behavior
Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **Harassment**: Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal attacks
- **Discrimination**: Public or private harassment based on protected characteristics
- **Privacy Violations**: Publishing others' private information without permission
- **Unethical Conduct**: Encouraging or engaging in unethical AI research practices
- **Legal Violations**: Promoting activities that violate terms of service or laws
- **Inappropriate Content**: Sharing content that is not related to the repository's purpose

## 🔬 Research Ethics

Given the sensitive nature of this repository, additional ethical standards apply:

### Responsible Research
- **Legitimate Sources**: Only contribute content obtained through legitimate means
- **Privacy Respect**: Protect the privacy of individuals in any shared content
- **Harm Prevention**: Consider the potential impact of disclosed information
- **Legal Compliance**: Ensure all contributions comply with applicable laws

### Academic Integrity
- **Accurate Attribution**: Provide proper attribution for all sources
- **Honest Representation**: Present information accurately and without modification
- **Transparent Methods**: Clearly document how information was obtained
- **Ethical Use**: Use repository content only for legitimate research/education

## 🛡️ Enforcement Responsibilities

Repository maintainers are responsible for:
- Clarifying standards of acceptable behavior
- Taking appropriate corrective action in response to violations
- Removing, editing, or rejecting contributions that violate this code of conduct
- Temporarily or permanently banning contributors for inappropriate behavior

## 📞 Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by:

1. **GitHub Issues**: For non-sensitive violations
2. **Private Contact**: Reach out to maintainers directly for serious matters
3. **Security Advisory**: Use GitHub's private reporting for security-related concerns

### What to Include in Reports
- Description of the incident
- Context and relevant links/screenshots
- Impact on you or the community
- Any previous attempts to address the issue

## ⚖️ Enforcement Guidelines

### 1. Warning
**Community Impact**: Minor violation of community standards.
**Consequence**: Private written warning with clarification of the violation.

### 2. Temporary Restriction
**Community Impact**: Moderate violation or continued minor violations.
**Consequence**: Temporary restriction from repository interactions.

### 3. Permanent Ban
**Community Impact**: Serious violation, harassment, or repeated violations.
**Consequence**: Permanent ban from all repository interactions.

## 🌍 Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All repository spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Public spaces when representing the project
- Private communications related to the project
- Any community events or gatherings

## 📚 Additional Guidelines

### For AI Research
- **Ethical Standards**: Follow established ethical guidelines for AI research
- **Responsible Disclosure**: Report concerning findings through appropriate channels
- **Impact Consideration**: Think about the broader implications of your contributions
- **Community Benefit**: Prioritize the benefit to the research community

### For Open Source Collaboration
- **Constructive Contributions**: Focus on improving the repository and its value
- **Respectful Disagreement**: Disagree respectfully and focus on ideas, not people
- **Inclusive Participation**: Welcome newcomers and help them contribute effectively
- **Quality Standards**: Maintain high standards for contributions and discussions

## 🔄 Updates and Changes

This Code of Conduct may be updated periodically to:
- Address new situations or challenges
- Incorporate community feedback
- Align with evolving ethical standards
- Comply with platform requirements
